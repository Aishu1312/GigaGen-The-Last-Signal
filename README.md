# 🎬 THE LAST SIGNAL

### GigaGen Competition | Round 1 — Comic | Round 2 — Video + Audio

> **When the world went silent, something beneath it started calling.**

---

## 🌌 Project Overview

**The Last Signal** is a cinematic science-fiction storytelling project created for the **GigaGen Competition**.

Set decades after the collapse of civilization, the story follows a survivor who discovers a mysterious signal emerging from a forgotten world. What begins as a search for its source leads to an ancient underground technology and a decision that could determine humanity's future.

The project transforms the story across two creative rounds — from a **visual comic narrative** to an **immersive video experience with audio**.

---

# 🏆 Competition Rounds

## 📖 Round 1 — Comic

The first round presents **The Last Signal** as a **10-page illustrated science-fiction comic**.

The comic establishes the post-apocalyptic world, introduces the mysterious signal, reveals the forgotten temple and the **Pran-Yantra**, and builds toward a choice between two possible futures for humanity.

### ✨ Highlights

- 10-page visual narrative
- Post-apocalyptic science-fiction setting
- Cinematic world-building
- Character-driven storytelling
- Mystery and technological discovery
- Humanity vs. technological progress
- A choice between **REBOOT** and **SURVIVE**


# 🎥 Round 2 — Video + Audio

The second round transforms the comic into a **cinematic video experience enhanced with audio**.

The video brings the visual story to life through motion, atmosphere and sound, creating a more immersive interpretation of the world introduced in Round 1.

### ✨ Highlights

- Cinematic visual storytelling
- Video adaptation of the comic narrative
- Atmospheric presentation
- Audio-enhanced storytelling
- Sci-fi mood and immersive experience
- Visual continuity between Round 1 and Round 2

# 🧩 Story at a Glance

### 🌍 The World

Thirty-seven years after the blackout, civilization has fallen silent.

### 📡 The Signal

A mysterious transmission suggests that something beneath the abandoned world is still alive.

### 🏔️ The Discovery

Following the signal leads to a hidden entrance beneath a mountain and a forgotten temple.

### ⚙️ The Pran-Yantra

Deep underground lies an ancient technology — the **Pran-Yantra** — sealed away by its guardians.

### ⚖️ The Choice

Humanity faces two possible futures:

**REBOOT**  
Restore technological civilization, but risk consuming the planet's remaining energy.

**SURVIVE**  
Remain technologically primitive and preserve humanity's future.

---

# 🎨 Creative Vision

**The Last Signal** explores a simple but powerful question:

> **If humanity had the power to rebuild civilization, would it be wise enough to use it?**

The project combines **science fiction, visual storytelling, mystery and human choice** to explore the relationship between technological advancement and responsibility.
