## 🎬 Project Overview

**The Last Signal** is a science-fiction comic created for the GigaGen competition.

The story follows a young survivor living decades after civilization has collapsed. 
When he discovers an old radio signal, he follows it through abandoned roads, 
ruined towers and a forgotten temple, eventually discovering an ancient underground 
technology known as the Pran-Yantra.

The discovery forces him to make a choice that could determine the future of humanity.

---

## 📖 Story

Thirty years after the world went silent, civilization has collapsed.

A mysterious signal begins broadcasting from the mountains.

The protagonist follows the signal and discovers a hidden underground temple 
containing an ancient machine.

The machine reveals a disturbing truth about humanity's past and presents 
two possible futures:

- REBOOT — Restore civilization and technological progress
- SURVIVE — Leave humanity technologically primitive

The protagonist must decide whether humanity is ready to use the power 
it once misused.
